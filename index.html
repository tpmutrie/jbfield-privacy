JB Field

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

JB Field is a field-audit app published by Johnson Barrow ("we", "us") for documenting variable frequency drives (VFDs), motors, and related equipment at facility sites. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and when it leaves your device.

In short: audit data and photos stay on your device. They only leave it when you choose to export or share them, or when you use the SharePoint upload feature. JB Field has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

Information the app handles

  • Session details you enter, such as site name, auditor name, facility guide name, and primary facility contact.
  • Equipment information, such as building and room locations, equipment IDs, equipment types, nameplate specifications, and notes.
  • Photos of equipment and nameplates that you take or select in the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or any information used to identify you across other apps or websites.

Where your data is stored

All audit data and photos are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to our servers in the background. Text recognition (OCR) of nameplate photos is performed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision technology. Photos are not sent anywhere to be read.

When data leaves your device

Data only leaves your device through actions you take:

  • Export and share. When you export to Excel or export photos, the files are handed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where they go (for example Files, Mail, or AirDrop).
  • SharePoint upload. When you choose "Upload to SharePoint," the session's audit data (site and contact names, equipment details, and nameplate data) is sent over an encrypted connection to a Microsoft 365 workflow operated by Johnson Barrow, which generates the audit report and stores it in Johnson Barrow's SharePoint. Photos are not included in this upload. That data is then handled under Microsoft's and Johnson Barrow's data policies.

Device permissions

  • Camera, to photograph equipment and nameplates during an audit.
  • Photo library, so you can attach existing photos to an audit.
  • Bluetooth, only to connect to Brady label printers for printing equipment labels, if you use that feature.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app. The app works without them, but the related features will be unavailable.

Data retention and deletion

Audit data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual equipment items or entire sessions inside the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Reports already uploaded to SharePoint, or files you have shared elsewhere, are kept according to the policies of wherever they were sent. Contact us to request removal of uploaded reports.

Children

JB Field is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
